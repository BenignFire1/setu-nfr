# AI Advertising Planner

## Smart Tool for Planning Railway Advertising Campaigns

The AI Advertising Planner helps businesses identify the best railway stations and advertising formats for their campaigns based on passenger traffic, station category, and campaign objectives.

The tool analyzes business requirements and recommends:

- Best railway stations
- Suitable advertising formats
- Estimated campaign reach
- Suggested advertising plan

---

# 1. Purpose

The AI tool helps businesses answer a key question:

**"Where should we advertise in railways to get the best reach?"**

It simplifies decision-making by using railway passenger data and station categories to generate optimized advertising recommendations.

# 3. AI Recommendation Engine

The system analyzes railway station data including:

- Passenger footfall
- Station category
- Regional relevance
- Advertising formats available

---

## 3.1 Station Data Example

| Station | Category | Annual Passengers |
|--------|----------|------------------|
| Guwahati | NSG-2 | 69,30,846 |
| New Jalpaiguri | NSG-2 | 47,67,787 |
| Katihar | NSG-2 | 64,45,323 |
| Silchar | NSG-6 | 13,65,000 |

---

## 3.2 AI Scoring Model

Each station receives a commercial score based on:

Station Score =  
Passenger Traffic (50%) +  
Station Category (30%) +  
Regional Relevance (20%)

Stations with higher scores have stronger advertising potential.
